Status and news

Project status and short news on the activity of the team

Modified

November 17, 2026

Project status

  1. Year 1 (2023–24)
  2. Year 2 (2024–25)
  3. Year 3 (2025–26)
  4. Year 4 (2026–27)
  5. Completed (Aug 2027)

Current status: in progress, fourth and last year (1 September 2026 – 31 August 2027). The R package giniVarCI is available on CRAN; OG1 (bias of the Gini index) has been achieved; the work on missing data (OG2) and applications (OG4) continues. Last update: 17 November 2026.

News

Stay informed. Receive the news and blog posts of GiniUs by email, or follow the RSS feed.

You can unsubscribe at any time. Your email is used only to send GiniUs news.

November 2026 Videos

Four new one-minute videos. Who runs the water service and what happens to the bill; whether women and men save water at home in the same way; the water services of small villages; and how precise official surveys are. Each video explains its link with inequality. Watch them.

November 2026 Project

Objective OG1 achieved. The first general objective of the project, identify and reduce the bias in studies based on the Gini index, has been completed. Its results are published in Sociological Methods & Research and Social Indicators Research and implemented in giniVarCI. See the conclusions.

November 2026 Open access

More articles in open access. 27 of the 48 articles of the project can now be read free of charge: 17 in DIGIBUG, the open repository of the University of Granada, and the rest in repositories of partner universities or open-access journals. Look for 🔓 in Publications.

November 2026 Videos

Our research in one minute. Eight one-minute videos explain, in plain language, the team’s articles on inequality and poverty: the Gini index, the “happy and poor” paradox, water and sanitation, tourism, young talents’ earnings and innovation. They are also published in the blog.

November 2026 Website

New GiniUs website. The project website moves to Quarto, with new interactive tools that run in the browser: a Gini index calculator, a missing data simulator (MCAR, MAR and MNAR), an interactive bias explorer, R exercises and an inequality observatory.

23 October 2026 Conference

IX EIEE (Ciudad Real). J.F. Muñoz Rosas chairs the session on Quantitative Methods at the IX Encuentro Internacional de Especialización para la Investigación en Economía y Empresa, hosted by the University of Castilla-La Mancha, and P.A. Cano-Chica presents the project paper on confidence intervals for the Foster–Greer–Thorbecke poverty indices. More information and photo.

10 September 2026 Workshop

Working group on inequality at the XVIII AECPA Congress (Granada). Members of the project organise the working group Inequality, measurement and public policies (GT 5.4), coordinated by S. López Ruiz with E. Álvarez Verdejo as discussant, with papers by J. Guardiola, J.F. Muñoz, F. González-Gómez, P.J. Moya and S. López Ruiz. Programme and papers.

September 2026 Project

The project enters its fourth and last year (September 2026 – August 2027).

April 2026 Publication

A practical guide to proper estimation and inference of the Gini index by avoiding often encountered methodological pitfalls, by J.F. Muñoz, J.M. Pavía and E. Álvarez-Verdejo, published in Social Indicators Research. DOI · Video

December 2025 Conference

The Gini index and giniVarCI at CrossMED 2025. J.F. Muñoz Rosas presented The Gini index in inequality research: theory and practice with the R package giniVarCI at the international conference Landscapes Across the Mediterranean (2nd edition), University of Granada, 9–11 December 2025. More.

June 2025 Conference

Water services and the right to water at the University of Jaén. F. González-Gómez and J.A. Rodríguez Martín presented two papers at the II International Congress The value of water: new perspectives in a global world (Jaén, 5–6 June 2025): one on the challenges of the water services of small municipalities of Granada and one on the human right to water from the indigenous worldview in Latin America. More.

March 2025 Conference

Three contributions on the Gini index in Paris. At ICMATAP-25 (7–8 March) and ICFM-25 (10–11 March), J.F. Muñoz Rosas presented results on skewness and confidence intervals, common pitfalls and the giniVarCI package, and a new weighted estimator that reduces the bias of the Gini index. More.

February 2025 Transfer

A tariff guide for Spanish water services. F. González-Gómez and D. Ortiz Rodríguez (University of Granada) wrote the update of the Tariff Guide for urban water services, funded by the Spanish Federation of Municipalities and Provinces (FEMP) and promoted by the FEMP and AEAS (now DAQUAS). It was presented at the FEMP headquarters in Madrid on 11 February 2025. More.

November 2024 Conference

VIII EIEE (Cartagena). Two papers by members of the project: common errors in the estimation of the Gini index and the giniVarCI library, and fast jackknife variances for Theil indices. More information and photo.

22 May 2024 Seminar

Seminar on the Gini index and the R package giniVarCI, organised by the research group EPIC at the University of Granada. See the photos.

January 2024 Software

The R package giniVarCI is published on CRAN (version 0.0.1-3). More information.

1 September 2023 Project

The GiniUs project starts, funded by the Spanish State Research Agency (PID2022-136235NB-I00).

2023 Publication

Exploring and correcting the bias in the estimation of the Gini measure of inequality, by J.F. Muñoz, P.J. Moya-Fernández and E. Álvarez-Verdejo, published online in Sociological Methods & Research. DOI · Video
