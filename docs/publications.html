Articles of the project

Our research in one minute. The articles on inequality and poverty have a short video that explains, in plain language, the question, the method and the main conclusions. Look for ▶ 1-min video next to the title.

The table lists the articles in JCR journals published by team members during the project (2024–2026) in the research areas of the project. Articles are marked according to the topics and transfer areas of the project: ⚖ inequality and poverty, 💧 water, ✈ tourism and 🏭 firms; an article can carry more than one mark. Click on a title to open the article (DOI or, if not available, Google Scholar). 🔓 DIGIBUG links to the version deposited in the open repository of the University of Granada and 🔓 Open access to other open versions (repositories of partner universities or open-access journals), and ▶ 1-min video to a one-minute video that explains the article in plain language. Quality indicators: JCR quartile of the journal in the year of publication and, where applicable, rating in the Chartered ABS Academic Journal Guide (AJG). Citations and online attention of each article are on the Impact page.
