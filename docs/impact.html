Using this evidence in research assessments

For team members: how to use these indicators (e.g. CNEAI research periods, sexenios)

The assessment of research periods is based on a narrative of the relevance and impact of each contribution, supported by a responsible use of quantitative indicators (DORA and CoARA principles), and values open access with persistent identifiers. For each article, this page offers evidence that can be cited in the narrative:

  1. Quality and visibility of the journal: JCR quartile in the year of publication and ABS rating (publications).
  2. Open access: link to the deposited version (Handle or DOI).
  3. Scientific impact: citations (Dimensions badge; check also Web of Science, Scopus and Google Scholar), always in context (field, year).
  4. Social impact: online attention (Altmetric: news, policy documents, blogs), one-minute video and its plays, blog posts, seminars and workshops where the results were presented (dissemination).
  5. Reuse: downloads of giniVarCI and use of the online tools based on the article.
  6. Context: the article is a result of the competitive project PID2022-136235NB-I00, with an interdisciplinary and international team.

Save a copy of this page as a PDF (button at the top) with the date of each assessment, and keep the original sources (certificates, programmes, links) for the period being assessed.
